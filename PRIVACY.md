# Privacy Policy for ModernVPN

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal user data.

- **No Logging**: We do not log your traffic, DNS queries, or IP addresses.
- **Permissions**: Only permissions strictly necessary for VPN functionality are requested (such as `android.permission.INTERNET`, `android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `android.permission.BIND_VPN_SERVICE`).
- **No Third-Party Trackers**: We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Your privacy is our priority.
